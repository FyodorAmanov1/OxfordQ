How it works · technical boundary

Major and minor use separately trained hierarchical POP909 quantum models. Each mode has an 8-state 3-qubit coarse PQC. If OTHER is sampled, a mode-specific 4-qubit resolver chooses among fifteen common chromatic classes plus RARE_OTHER.

Each new mode/context is measured on the Qollab-selected backend with one circuit per backend submission. Measured histograms are cached separately for major and minor. “In-key” and “Adventurous” are transparent classical post-filters applied after measurement; “Authentic” uses the measured coarse distribution unchanged.

Major parameters were trained on POP909 major-key sections and minor parameters were trained independently on POP909 minor-key sections. Browser sound synthesis, transposition, random sampling from returned histograms and the rare-class fallback are classical. No quantum advantage or musical-quality advantage is claimed.
