How it works · technical boundary

The 3-qubit POP909 PQC represents eight coarse harmonic classes: I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii° and OTHER. If OTHER is sampled, a 4-qubit resolver chooses among fifteen common chromatic classes plus RARE_OTHER.

Each new context is measured on the Qollab-selected backend with one circuit per backend submission. The measured histogram is cached for the page session. “Diatonic” and “Chromatic” are transparent classical post-filters applied after measurement; “Original” uses the measured coarse distribution unchanged.

The model was trained on major-key sections only. Browser sound synthesis, transposition to the chosen key, random sampling from returned histograms and the rare-class fallback are classical. No quantum advantage or musical-quality advantage is claimed.
