How composition works

A six-qubit quantum walk runs once on your selected simulator. Its measured joint interval distribution supplies the initial contour amplitudes. A persistent 13-qubit ideal Qiskit state then evolves on 8,192 musical possibilities: melodic contour × attack rhythm × harmony. Coupled phases and interference change their joint probabilities as the piece develops.

The complete state simulation uses ideal, non-disturbing access to probabilities. It is calculated in browser Python, separately from the selected simulator. Classical musical rules choose register and chord voicings, maintain a four-bar theme, control variation, and close on the tonic. Acoustic piano samples and the backing instruments are rendered classically.

This is a creative generative mechanism. We do not claim a quantum speedup or superior musical quality.

One selected-backend job per run. To compose again, use Qollab’s Stop → Run. Outputs are newly generated, not a recorded soundtrack. No API key or paid hardware is required.
